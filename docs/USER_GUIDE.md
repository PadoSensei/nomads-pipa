## 1. Welcome to Nomads Pipa

Nomads Pipa is a local discovery and community platform designed to help digital nomads make the most of their time in Pipa, Brazil.

Use it to:

- Discover restaurants, cafés, bars, and local services.
- Find accommodation and transportation options.
- See what's happening today and meet other people.
- Discover sports, activities, and experiences.
- Join quests and unlock digital rewards.
- Access partner offers and vouchers.
- Find useful information about living and traveling in the area.

Nomads Pipa is more than a directory. It connects local discovery with community participation and rewards.

## 2. Getting Started

### 2.1 Open Nomads Pipa

Nomads Pipa is a mobile-first Progressive Web App (PWA).

Open the platform using its web address in your mobile browser. If supported by your device, you can add it to your home screen for easier access.

Installation is optional. You can use the platform through your browser.

### 2.2 Create an Account

Some features may be available without an account. An account is required for personalized features such as:

- Saving progress.
- Joining quests.
- Maintaining an inventory of unlocked rewards.
- Claiming eligible vouchers.
- Tracking quest history.

The registration flow should collect only the information necessary to create and operate the account.

### 2.3 Complete Your Profile

Your profile may include:

- Display name
- Profile picture
- Preferred language
- General interests
- Community preferences

Only provide information you are comfortable sharing. Review your privacy settings before making profile information public.

The community calendar helps answer:

**"If I want to meet people today, where should I go?"**

Explore upcoming activities and events, including community gatherings, live music, and local cultural events.

### 4.2 Understand Event Labels

Nomads Pipa uses three event classifications:

| Label               | Meaning                                                                        |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Nomads Pipa Meet-up | An official event organized or endorsed by the Nomads Pipa community team      |
| Community Pick      | A third-party event where community members have confirmed they plan to attend |

A Community Pick is not an official Nomads Pipa event. Attendance by community members may change.

## 5. Quests and Gamification

### 5.1 What Are Quests?

Quests encourage you to discover Pipa by completing activities at participating businesses and locations.

Examples may include visiting a partner, scanning a QR code, or completing a sequence of activities.

Each quest has its own requirements, validity period, and reward conditions.

### 5.2 Quest Tree

Your Quest Tree displays your available quest paths and progress.

Depending on the quest, you may see:

- Locked activities
- Available activities
- Activities in progress
- Completed activities
- Unlocked rewards

Follow the displayed requirements to progress.

A scan does not automatically complete every quest. The platform checks whether the activity meets the quest's rules.

### 5.3 Quest Progress

Your progress is recorded after the platform validates an eligible action.

If an activity does not register:

1. Check the quest requirements.
2. Confirm that the quest is still active.
3. Check whether the platform displayed a successful scan confirmation.
4. Refresh the app.
5. Contact support if the issue remains.

## 6. Your Digital Inventory

Your inventory stores digital items you have unlocked through participation.

It may include:

- Digital badges
- Collectible artwork
- Quest achievements
- Special rewards
- Status-related items

Unlocked items should remain associated with your account so you can view your collection later.

Some rewards may have additional eligibility or redemption conditions. Check the item details before visiting a partner.

## 7. QR Scanning

### 7.1 Why Scan a QR Code?

Participating businesses may display physical QR codes for quests, rewards, or other eligible activities.

Scanning allows the platform to validate a specific interaction.

### 7.2 How to Scan

1. Open the relevant quest, offer, or QR scanning feature.
2. Allow camera access when prompted.
3. Point your camera at the displayed QR code.
4. Wait for server-side validation.
5. Read the confirmation message.

If location verification is required for a specific activity, the app may request location permission.

Location permission should be requested only when needed for the feature. It may be denied, although some location-dependent activities may then be unavailable.

### 7.3 Scan Results

You may see one of the following:

- **Success:** The interaction was validated.
- **Already Used:** The QR code or reward has already been redeemed.
- **Expired:** The relevant activity is no longer active.
- **Not Eligible:** The activity's requirements were not met.
- **Invalid:** The QR code could not be verified.
- **Try Again:** A temporary connection or scanning problem occurred.

A successful scan may update quest progress, unlock a reward, or record an eligible interaction. The exact result depends on the activity.

## 8. Vouchers and the Digital Welcome Pack

### 8.1 Your Voucher Wallet

The voucher wallet keeps your available offers organized.

It may include:

- Active vouchers
- Claimed offers
- Redeemed vouchers
- Expired vouchers
- Flash Offers

Each voucher should show its conditions and validity period.

### 8.2 How to Redeem a Voucher

1. Open your voucher wallet.
2. Select an active voucher.
3. Read the redemption conditions.
4. Visit the participating business.
5. Follow the displayed redemption instructions.
6. Present the required QR code or voucher information to authorized staff.
7. Wait for confirmation.

Do not assume a voucher has been redeemed until the platform confirms it.

### 8.3 Digital Welcome Pack

The Digital Welcome Pack brings together selected offers from participating businesses.

It is intended to help new arrivals discover local businesses and access useful benefits.

Available offers may vary over time.

## 9. Flash Offers

Flash Offers are time-limited promotions from participating businesses.

A Flash Offer may:

- Be available only for a limited period.
- Target users within a nearby radius.
- Have a limited number of claims.
- Expire when the time limit or claim limit is reached.

For example, a business may release an offer for 90 minutes to the first 10 eligible users within a 2 km radius.

### Claiming a Flash Offer

1. Open the Flash Offer.
2. Check the benefit and conditions.
3. Confirm that you are eligible.
4. Claim the offer while it is available.
5. Visit the business before expiration.
6. Follow the redemption instructions.

Claiming an offer and redeeming it are different actions. A claim does not automatically mean the benefit has been used.

## 10. Golden QR and Special Rewards

Some activities may offer enhanced rewards to users who meet specific status or eligibility requirements.

A Golden QR may represent a special reward or higher-tier benefit.

The exact eligibility rules and benefits should be displayed in the relevant activity.

Do not assume that every QR scan produces points or a reward. Each activity has its own validation and reward rules.

## 11. Concierge Services

Nomads Pipa may offer personalized assistance for users who want help organizing their stay or finding suitable local services.

Concierge packages may include different levels of assistance, such as:

- Local orientation
- Accommodation assistance
- Transportation coordination
- Personalized recommendations
- Curated experiences
- More comprehensive stay planning

Available packages, prices, and conditions should be reviewed before purchasing.

Concierge services are distinct from ordinary directory listings and self-service discovery.

## 12. Profile, Privacy, and Account Safety

### 12.1 Keep Your Account Secure

- Use a secure password where password authentication is available.
- Do not share login credentials.
- Sign out on shared devices.
- Report suspicious account activity.
- Avoid sharing sensitive information in public profile fields.

### 12.2 Location and Camera Permissions

Some features need camera or location access.

You can manage these permissions through your device or browser settings.

Camera access is needed to scan QR codes. Location access may be used for nearby offers or specific verification requirements.

### 12.3 Personal Information

Nomads Pipa should collect and use personal information according to its published privacy policy.

Your account settings should make it possible to review available profile information and manage supported privacy preferences.

## 13. Troubleshooting

| Problem                          | What to do                                                         |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| App does not load                | Check your internet connection and reload the page                 |
| Cannot sign in                   | Check your credentials and use the available account recovery flow |
| Camera does not open             | Review browser camera permissions                                  |
| QR code does not scan            | Improve lighting and reposition the camera                         |
| Scan is unsuccessful             | Check the activity conditions and retry                            |
| Quest progress is missing        | Confirm the activity was eligible and validated                    |
| Voucher is unavailable           | Check its expiration, status, and conditions                       |
| Flash Offer has expired          | Look for another active offer                                      |
| Event details seem incorrect     | Confirm with the event organizer                                   |
| Business information is outdated | Contact the platform or the business                               |

## 14. Getting Help

Contact Nomads Pipa support for:

- Account access problems
- Missing quest progress
- QR scanning issues
- Voucher disputes
- Incorrect directory information
- Event corrections
- Privacy questions
- General platform feedback

When reporting a problem, include the relevant activity or voucher name and an approximate time. Do not send your password or unnecessary personal information.
